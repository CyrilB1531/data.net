# 0325 — Three namespace indexes answer 'which one do I want' without the diagram

**Issue:** [#0325](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/0325) · **Status:** accepted — **retrospective**, written 2026-08-29 from the commits that closed it · **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Problem

Nine namespace indexes hand the reader a flowchart under the heading *"which one do I want"*. Three asked the question and answered it in prose only. The inconsistency is the defect: a reader who has learned to look for the diagram finds nothing on exactly the three pages with the most types.

## What was decided

The three get the diagram their siblings have — [#326](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/326), [#327](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/327), [#328](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/328), one each. **In every case the prose already contained the routing; nothing had assembled it.**

A diagram is removed in review when it only restates the sentence above it, which is why each of the three had to find a branch the prose was not already drawing.
