# KruskalWallis

The Kruskal-Wallis H test: the rank-based k-sample comparison.

What [`OneWayAnova`](onewayanova.md) is to [`TTest.Independent`](ttest-independent.md), this is
to [`MannWhitney`](mannwhitney.md): on two groups it agrees with Mann-Whitney's asymptotic
two-sided p-value.

## Members

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [`KruskalWallis.Test`](kruskalwallis-test.md) | Compares two or more groups by their ranks in the pooled sample. |
