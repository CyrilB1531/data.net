# RocAuc

The metric for a model that outputs a score rather than a decision: how often it ranks a positive
above a negative.

## Members

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [`RocAuc.Score`](rocauc-score.md) | Area under the ROC curve for two classes. |
| [`RocAuc.MultiClass`](rocauc-multiclass.md) | Area under the ROC curve for more than two classes, by reducing to binary problems. |
