# Completeness

The mirror of `Homogeneity`: this is that score with the two labellings exchanged. Merging everything into one cluster scores `1` here and `0` there, so the two are read together or not at all.

## Members

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [`Completeness.Score`](completeness-score.md) | Whether every sample of one class landed in the same cluster. |
