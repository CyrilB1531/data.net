# MeanSquaredLogError

Squared error on `log(1 + y)`: the metric for counts and quantities where being out by a factor
matters more than being out by an amount.

## Members

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [`MeanSquaredLogError.Score`](meansquaredlogerror-score.md) | The mean of the squared differences of `log(1 + y)`. |
| [`MeanSquaredLogError.PerOutput`](meansquaredlogerror-peroutput.md) | One mean squared log error per output, unreduced. |
